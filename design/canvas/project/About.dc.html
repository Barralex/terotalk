Quién enseña

The proof section of the home page, and the only one the visitor sets in motion. Three short paragraphs at 1.25rem over a 52ch measure, a statement that writes itself in, four facts with a scene each, and the three roles on a sheet. It has to fit one screen, so the padding is the short one and the copy is cut rather than shrunk.
